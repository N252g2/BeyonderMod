# Beyonder (mod)

The NeoForge 1.21.1 mod behind **Beyonder**, a Lord of the Mysteries inspired MMORPG for Minecraft.
It ships to players through the [Beyonder Launcher](https://github.com/N252g2/BeyonderLauncher) pack.

## What's in it so far: Tingen at Night

- **Color grade**: a muted Victorian palette with cool shadows, warm gaslight highlights, vignette and film grain.
- **Fog and night tint**: industrial haze by day; close, cold fog by night.
- **Red moon**: an original eight-phase moon texture.
- **Street blocks**: gas lamp, lamp post, Tingen cobbles, soot-stained bricks (Beyonder creative tab).
- **Spirit Vision** (default key **V**): the world washes into watercolor. Players and mobs show a faint halo whose
  size, colour and pulse follow how strong they are. Halos are animated: wisps stream off them, faster for stronger spirits,
  and marker beams carry rising bands of light. Halo colours run pale → teal → gold → crimson → violet; hostile ones lean red.
  You can sense halos faintly through walls. Spirit markers (waypoints) appear as columns of light with a label;
  test with `/spiritmark <name>` and `/spiritmark clear`.

Visual settings are in *Mods > Beyonder > Config* or `config/beyonder-client.toml`.

## The Seer pathway (0.3.0)

- **Potions**: Seer (Sequence 9), Clown (8), Magician (7). Drinking the Seer potion makes you a Beyonder.
  Advance only to the next Sequence, and only once the current potion is **fully digested**; drinking early,
  skipping a Sequence or mixing pathways builds **loss of control**. At 100% you are consumed.
- **Spirituality** powers your abilities and regenerates over time. Spirit Vision (V) is a Seer ability and
  drains it while open; running dry closes the vision and unsettles you.
- **Acting** digests the potion: as a Seer, divine and observe with Spirit Vision.
- **Spirit Pendulum**: rename it in an anvil to ask a question (a player's name, `village`, `home`, or an ore
  like `diamond`). It swings toward the answer and leaves a spirit marker. Costs 15 Spirituality.
- **HUD**: your Sequence, Spirituality, digestion and loss of control, top left.
- Halos in Spirit Vision blaze brighter for higher-Sequence Beyonders.
- Test commands (ops): `/beyonder set <player> seer [sequence]`, `/beyonder digestion <player> <percent>`,
  `/beyonder spirituality <player> <amount>`, `/beyonder calm <player>`; anyone: `/beyonder status`.

## Ability wheel, potion visions and buildings (0.5.0)

- **Ability wheel**: hold **G**, point at a power (or Spirit Vision), let go. **R** uses the chosen power.
- **Potion visions**: drinking plays a vision: an icy chill, the world swirling and splitting, a racing
  heart and whispers, then a flash at the climax when the outcome lands (red darkness if it goes wrong).
- **Buildings** (ops): `/beyonder build <terrace_house | terrace_house_b | corner_shop | street | chapel | tingen_street>`
  places a building two blocks ahead, front facing you. Designs live in `tools/gen_structures.py`
  (`python3 tools/gen_structures.py --preview out/` renders previews).

## Beyonder Shaders

`shaderpack/` is our own Iris shader pack (Standard edition): soft shadows, hazy sun, red moonlight, flickering
gaslight, coal-smoke fog, lamp bloom and the Tingen grade. CI checks every program with glslang and publishes
`BeyonderShaders-<version>.zip` beside the jar. `python3 tools/check_shaders.py` checks it locally.

## Building

Push to GitHub: Actions builds the jar. Every push to `main` publishes a pre-release with the jar, the shader pack and `release.json` (hashes).
Locally: `./gradlew build` (Java 21), or `./gradlew runClient` to test.

Textures are generated by `tools/gen_textures.py` (Python 3 + Pillow). All art is original.
