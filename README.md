# Beyonder (mod)

The NeoForge 1.21.1 mod behind **Beyonder**, a Lord of the Mysteries inspired MMORPG for Minecraft.
It ships to players through the [Beyonder Launcher](https://github.com/N252g2/BeyonderLauncher) pack.

## What's in it so far: Tingen at Night

- **Color grade**: a muted Victorian palette with cool shadows, warm gaslight highlights, vignette and film grain.
- **Fog and night tint**: industrial haze by day; close, cold fog by night.
- **Red moon**: an original eight-phase moon texture.
- **Street blocks**: gas lamp, lamp post, Tingen cobbles, soot-stained bricks (Beyonder creative tab).
- **Spirit Vision** (default key **V**): the world washes into watercolor. Players and mobs show a faint halo whose
  size, colour and pulse follow how strong they are (pale → teal → gold → crimson → violet; hostile ones lean red).
  You can sense halos faintly through walls. Spirit markers (waypoints) appear as columns of light with a label;
  test with `/spiritmark <name>` and `/spiritmark clear`.

Visual settings are in *Mods > Beyonder > Config* or `config/beyonder-client.toml`.

## Beyonder Shaders

`shaderpack/` is our own Iris shader pack (Standard edition): soft shadows, hazy sun, red moonlight, flickering
gaslight, coal-smoke fog, lamp bloom and the Tingen grade. CI checks every program with glslang and publishes
`BeyonderShaders-<version>.zip` beside the jar. `python3 tools/check_shaders.py` checks it locally.

## Building

Push to GitHub: Actions builds the jar. Every push to `main` publishes a pre-release with the jar, the shader pack and `release.json` (hashes).
Locally: `./gradlew build` (Java 21), or `./gradlew runClient` to test.

Textures are generated by `tools/gen_textures.py` (Python 3 + Pillow). All art is original.
